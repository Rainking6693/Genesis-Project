# TOP 15 BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES

## Scoring Methodology Overview

This report highlights the top 15 highest-scoring businesses from our comprehensive evaluation. Each business was scored on a 1-100 scale across 6 criteria:

- **Problem/Pain Points** (25%): How well they solve real problems
- **Target Audience** (20%): Market size potential
- **Competition** (15%): Level of market competition
- **Demand** (15%): Market demand indicators
- **Social Validation** (10%): Social proof and engagement
- **Feasibility** (15%): Technical and business viability
